Exciting Expeditions Rafting Co.

At Exciting Expeditions, our whitewater rafting trips are designed to provide thrill-seekers with an unforgettable experience on the water. Our expert guides ensure safety while you navigate through stunning landscapes and challenging rapids.

History

Exciting Expeditions was founded in 1995 by a group of outdoor enthusiasts who wanted to share their love for river adventures. Starting with just two rafts and a passion for exploration, our company has grown into a premier whitewater rafting outfit focused on safety, sustainability, and fun.
